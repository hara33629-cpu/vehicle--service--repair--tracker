Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Digital Passbook work?

The Digital Passbook compiles all logged service dates, odometer readings, replaced parts, and invoices into an official printable document you can present to car buyers for higher resale value.

Can I track electric vehicles (EVs)?

Yes! AutoTrack PRO supports EV charging logs, battery state-of-health tracking, miles-per-kWh efficiency calculations, and HEPA filter array replacement intervals.

Is my data backed up?

Your garage data is automatically stored in local storage and can be exported at any time as a CSV file using the Export CSV button in the topbar.
