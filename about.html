About AutoTrack PRO

We build precision automotive tracking software for car enthusiasts, mechanics, and modern fleet operators.

Our Engineering Mission

AutoTrack PRO was created out of a desire to eliminate maintenance ambiguity. Traditional paper service logs and clunky dealer receipts get lost, leading to missed oil flushes, voided warranties, and depreciated vehicle resale value.

Our platform brings modern telemetry analytics, digital passbooks, and smart mileage notifications to every car in your garage.

Built for All Powertrains

  • ✓ Gasoline & High-RPM Engines: Synthetic oil changes, spark plug gaps, timing belts.
  • ✓ Electric EV Systems: Battery health, HEPA filter array, coolant loop, miles-per-kWh logs.
  • ✓ Hybrid Powertrains: Dual engine/inverter cooling systems and brake regeneration checks.
